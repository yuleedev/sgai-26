# Team Viral

Team Viral is a strategy game built in Godot where players manage a city of 25 neighborhoods during a zombie outbreak. Each day you choose to treat, clear, or wait, balancing limited doses and funding while racing to protect as much of the population as possible before a vaccine arrives. The game was made as a project for the MIT Beaverworks Summer Institute's Serious Games with AI course, created by Aadvik Kesarwani, Annabelle Pan, Aydin Heugh, Hannah Ying, and Yulee Oh.

## Getting Started/Installation

Mac and Windows downloads are available here:
https://yuleedev.itch.io/team-viral

After downloading:

- **macOS:** Extract the downloaded file and launch the app.
- **Windows:** Extract the downloaded file and run the .exe.

## Controls

- **Mouse:** Click cells on the map and use the on screen menus and buttons for all actions.

## How to Play

- Each day, pick one neighborhood and one action: **Treat** (cure some infected using a dose), **Clear** (kill some infected, free but morally heavy), or **Wait** (skip the day).
- Watch your funding and dose count. Spend funding on upgrades between actions to boost treatment, clearing, doses, or slow the spread.
- Infection spreads from neighborhood to neighborhood over time, so untreated cells put their neighbors at risk.
- The game ends when the vaccine arrives or when all 24 neighborhoods are fully infected or dead.
- After the game, you'll see three separate scores: people lost, zombies killed, and zombies cured.
